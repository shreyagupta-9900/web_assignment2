Proposed Solution & Core Features

SMART INDIA
HACKATHON
2024
❖
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Tailored interfaces for 3 distinct roles: System Admin (Asset CRUD), Lab In-Charge (Requisition Approval & Dispatch), and Requester (Catalog browsing & checkout requests).
❖
4-Stage Requisition Lifecycle: 1) Request raised with purpose & future date ➔ 2) Lab In-Charge review/approval ➔ 3) Physical issue dispatch with automated stock decrement ➔ 4) Return audit with condition logging.
❖
Stock Integrity & Safety Guardrails: Atomic validation prevents issuing more units than physically available. Admin is restricted from deleting or downscaling items currently checked out.
❖
Return Condition Auditing (OK / Damaged / Lost): Upon physical return, In-Charge audits equipment condition: OK replenishes stock, Damaged schedules maintenance, and Lost adjusts total inventory count.
❖
Automated Overdue Detection & Alerts: Dynamic date comparison flags unreturned gear past deadline with pulsing dashboard alert banners.
